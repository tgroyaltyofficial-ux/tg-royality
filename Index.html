Welcome to TG Family

A community built on loyalty, discipline, respect and unity.

Every member is expected to respect the community and follow its official rules. Read the rules carefully and help maintain a strong and united family.

♜ Community Rules

  1. Respect: Treat every member with dignity. Harassment and bullying are prohibited.
  2. Loyalty: Act responsibly and protect the trust of the TG Fam.
  3. Privacy: Do not share private messages or personal information without permission.
  4. No Spam: Avoid flooding chats, repeated promotions and unwanted links.
  5. No Unnecessary Fights: Resolve disagreements calmly. Contact an admin when necessary.
  6. Follow Admin Decisions: Raise concerns respectfully through the proper channel.
  7. Stay Relevant: Follow the purpose and guidelines of the group.
  8. 10-Day Name Change Rule: All TG Fam members must change their names within 10 days. Members must include the official TG tag in their names and follow the naming format set by the administration.
  9. Temporary Suspension for Personal Matters: Any TG Fam member involved in personal matters unrelated to the TG Fam may be temporarily suspended from the family. Once all issues have been resolved, the member may be recruited back into the family.

♛ Royal Ranks

01 — Founder

Leads the community and makes final decisions.

02 — Royal Council

Helps manage the community, resolve disputes and guide admins.

03 — Admin

Enforces rules, assists members and moderates discussions.

04 — Elite Member

A trusted and active member who contributes positively.

05 — Member

A valued member of TG ROYALITY who follows the rules.

Rank assignments are decided by the group's leadership.

⚖️ Punishment Policy

  1. First minor offence: Reminder or warning.
  2. Repeated offence: Formal warning or temporary mute.
  3. Serious or repeated misconduct: Temporary suspension.
  4. Severe violations: Removal from the group after review.
  5. Failure to follow the 10-day name-change rule may result in administrative action.

Members should have a fair opportunity to explain their side. Serious threats or privacy violations may require immediate action.
