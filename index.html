01 / Rule Book

Respect

Treat every member fairly. Harassment, threats and personal attacks are not allowed.

Loyalty & Unity

Respect community decisions and report serious issues to authorised leaders.

Communication

Avoid spam, impersonation and sharing private conversations without permission.

Responsibility

Follow platform rules, protect privacy and resolve disagreements peacefully.

Membership means agreeing to follow these community standards.

02 / Ranks & Leadership

♛ Leader

Oversees the community and approves major decisions.

◆ Co-Leader

Supports the leader and helps manage the group.

✦ Administrator

Helps moderate discussions and handle reports fairly.

★ Senior Member

Supports newcomers and sets a positive example.

● Member

Participates respectfully and follows the rule book.

03 / Discipline System

Level 1 — Warning

For minor first-time violations.

Level 2 — Final Warning

For repeated misconduct.

Level 3 — Temporary Suspension

For continued or more serious violations, following a review.

Level 4 — Removal

For serious or repeated violations after a fair review.

Members should receive a reason for moderation decisions and a way to appeal them.
