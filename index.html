Welcome to the Royal Family

A community built on loyalty, discipline, respect and unity.

Every member is expected to respect the community and its members.

♜ Community Rules

  1. Respect: Treat every member with dignity. No harassment or bullying.
  2. Loyalty: Act responsibly and protect the group's trust.
  3. Privacy: Never share private messages or personal information without permission.
  4. No spam: Avoid flooding chats, repeated promotions or unwanted links.
  5. No unnecessary fights: Resolve disagreements calmly. Contact an admin when needed.
  6. Follow admin decisions: Raise concerns respectfully through the proper channel.
  7. Temporary Suspension: Any member of the TG Fam who becomes involved in personal matters unrelated to the TG Fam may be temporarily suspended. Once all issues have been resolved, the member may be recruited back into the family.
  8. Stay relevant: Follow the group's purpose and topic guidelines.

♛ Royal Ranks

01 — Founder / King

Leads the community and makes final decisions.

02 — Royal Council

Helps manage the community, resolve disputes and guide admins.

03 — Admin

Enforces rules, assists members and moderates discussions.

04 — Elite Member

A trusted and active member who contributes positively.

05 — Member

A valued member of TG ROYALITY who follows the rules.

Rank assignments are decided by the group's leadership.

⚖️ Punishment Policy

  1. First minor offence: Friendly reminder or warning.
  2. Repeated offence: Formal warning or temporary mute.
  3. Serious or repeated misconduct: Temporary suspension.
  4. Severe violations: Removal from the group after review.

Members should have a fair opportunity to explain their side. Serious threats or privacy violations may require immediate action.
